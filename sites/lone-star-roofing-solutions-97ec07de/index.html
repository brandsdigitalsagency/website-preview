Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our roofing companies services, timelines, and pricing policies.

How do I know if my roof needs a complete replacement or a simple repair? ↓

If shingles are curling, missing across large sections, or older than 20 years, replacement is typically more economical than patch repairs. We provide a photographic inspection showing exact roof conditions so you can make an informed choice.

Can you help with insurance storm damage claims? ↓

Yes. We document hail fractures and wind uplift with high-resolution imagery and meet your insurance adjuster on-site to ensure all storm damage is accurately represented.

How long does a standard residential roof replacement take? ↓

Most residential re-roofing projects are completed in 1 to 2 days, including full tear-off, underlayment installation, shingle application, and magnetic nail sweep.

Do you serve all neighborhoods in Dallas? ↓

Yes, we provide full roofing, gutter, and repair services across all residential and commercial zones in Dallas and surrounding communities.
